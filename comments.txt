In serverless environments (like Vercel), 'fire and forget' promises that are not awaited may be terminated prematurely when the response is sent. This makes the email notification unreliable. To ensure the notification is sent, you should `await` the call, even if it slightly increases the redirect latency, or use a background task mechanism like `request.waitUntil` if supported by your deployment platform.

```typescript
          const { sendNewRegistrationNotification } = await import('@/lib/email')
          await sendNewRegistrationNotification(displayName, email).catch(console.error)
```
